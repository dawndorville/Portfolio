Meet Pixel

Bringing a brand character to life with motion.

Q3 2025

Character introduction for social

This was the first public-facing project that AI was used for. The social team came to us with a request to formally introduce one of our brand characters, Pixel, to our social account in a fun way. The result was a reality-show-inspired introduction, assembled from generated imagery, animation, voiceover, sound effects, and music.

The approach

Direct the sequence across tools.

01

Set the visual direction

Using Nano Banana in Figma, I created the starting frame for each cut, highlighting one aspect of Pixel’s character.

02

Build the motion and voice

I brought the first frames to Midjourney to add animation and created the voiceover in ElevenLabs.

03

Shape the finished piece

Finally in After Effects, I brought the animated cuts together with music and sound effects to form the final social introduction.

giveaway

threat hunting cat game

We wanted to take this campaign another step further and create a quick, digital giveaway. A threat-hunting video for cats extended the campaign as a digital giveaway. When the initial idea of a custom screensaver did not work for the project, the team published the video on YouTube.
