Meet Pixel

Brand character social debut

Q3 2025

social

This was the first public-facing project that AI was used for. The social team came to us with an opportunity to formally introduce one of our brand characters, Pixel, to our social account in a fun way and this was the end result. The goal was to make this feel like an upbeat reality show-like character introduction. I used Nano Banana to create the first frame for each of the cuts, then dropped them into Midjourney for motion. I created the voiceover in Eleven Labs and compiled all the animated pieces together with sound effects and music.

Visuals: Nano Banana, Midjourney
Voiceover: Eleven Labs

threat hunting cat game

We wanted to take this campaign another step further and create a quick, digital giveaway. The idea was to create a screensaver that would function as a threat-hunting game for cats. Unfortunately, it wasn't possible to set a video as a screensaver so we pivoted and uploaded the video to Youtube. Cats loved it.
