VTL Capture the Flag

campaign for the Varonis threat research team game

Breach at the Beach is a story-driven CTF created by Varonis Threat Labs that drops security practitioners into a real-world cloud identity breach investigation.

visuals

Storm scene transition

This scene was the basis for the look of the game. In this scene, Pixel (VTL team mascot and threat hunter), is visiting the beach when a storm rolls in. The characters were drawn pixel by pixel and the more complex scenery elements were created with Nano Banana, then running the visuals through a Figma Make program I created that would turn them into vectorized pixels and provide SVG downloads.

Game branding

Breach at the Beach featured a fully designed game experience complete with characters and it's own maze chase arcade style mini game.
